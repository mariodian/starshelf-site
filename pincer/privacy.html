Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026 · Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pincer (“Pincer,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Pincer desktop application for macOS, Windows, and Linux (the “App”), the optional pincerd daemon (the “Daemon”), and this website (the “Site”). Monitoring data stays on devices and hosts you control. Pincer runs no cloud that receives agent health payloads by default.

1. Who we are

Pincer is open-source tray monitoring for local AI agents. It shows live status, history, charts, incidents, and reports from the system tray so agent health stays in view beside your work.

Contact: GitHub Issues and @mariodian on X.

2. Summary

  • Agent monitoring data lives on your device in local SQLite and related app data directories.
  • Agent health payloads stay on hosts you control. Pincer runs no cloud that receives them by default.
  • The App contacts agent and health endpoints you configure, and may check for updates you enable.
  • This Site is static. It ships no third-party analytics, ad pixels, or tracking cookies.

3. Information the App stores

On your machine, the App may store data in local SQLite and the application data directory, including:

  • Agent configuration — names, types, health endpoints, and setup you provide
  • Health check results and history — status samples, timestamps, incidents, and chart history
  • Settings — polling intervals, retention, notifications, launch-at-login, theme, auto-update preferences, and similar options
  • Secrets you set for local use — for example a Daemon auth secret stored where the App or Daemon expects it on your system

That data stays under your control on hosts you run. Pincer runs no multi-tenant backend that collects it for us.

4. Network activity

As you configure and use Pincer, the App or Daemon may:

  • Contact agent or health endpoints you configure (local or remote URLs you supply) to collect status
  • Check for updates from GitHub Releases or other update channels you enable
  • Talk to a Daemon (pincerd) that you host, using the secret you configure

Those parties process requests under their own policies and your setup. Once traffic leaves your machine under a configuration you chose, those systems control how they handle it.

5. Daemon

The optional Daemon (pincerd) keeps collecting health data after you close the desktop UI. It runs on a machine or server you control. You set and protect the authentication secret. Pincer runs no multi-tenant Daemon service for you.

If you expose the Daemon on a network, treat the secret and host like any other self-hosted service: restrict access, use TLS where appropriate, and rotate credentials if they leak.

6. This website

The Site is static (for example on GitHub Pages). It offers product information, this Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.

  • This Site has no forms that collect personal information.
  • This Site ships no third-party analytics, ad pixels, or tracking cookies.
  • Hosts (GitHub Pages, CDN, or DNS) may log IP address, user agent, and request URL. The host controls that logging; Pincer has no application backend for it.

7. Children’s privacy

Pincer targets adults and professionals, not children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Retention and deletion

Monitoring data and settings live in local SQLite and app data on devices you control, so you decide retention. You may:

  • Change retention and related settings in the App
  • Delete or reset App data directories and SQLite files
  • Uninstall the App and remove remaining app data if your OS leaves any behind
  • Stop or remove any Daemon you run and delete its data on that host

Pincer keeps no central user-account database for the App.

9. Security

Pincer is local-first: secrets and health history stay where you install the App or Daemon. Protect your device, OS account, disk encryption, backup copies of app data, and any Daemon secret or network exposure you configure. Perfect security does not exist.

10. International users

The App and Daemon run where you install them. Endpoints and update hosts you use may process requests in several countries under their rules. The host may serve this Site from more than one region.

11. Your choices and rights

Your jurisdiction may grant rights over personal data (access, deletion, correction, and more). For data only on your device or self-hosted Daemon, use the App controls and file system actions above. For data held by third-party agents, endpoints, or hosts you configure, contact those parties.

Privacy questions about Pincer: GitHub Issues or @mariodian.

12. Open source

App and Daemon source code is at github.com/mariodian/pincer. Review how local storage and network calls work there.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, the “Last updated” date at the top changes. Continued use of the App, Daemon, or Site after a change is acceptance of the revised policy where the law allows.

14. Contact

Privacy inquiries and reports: GitHub Issues
On X: @mariodian

This policy supports transparency for a local-first open-source product. It is not legal advice. For advice about your situation, consult a qualified lawyer.
